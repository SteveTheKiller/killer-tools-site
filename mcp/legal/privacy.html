KillerTools hosted tools privacy policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

What this policy covers

Steve the Killer operates the hosted KillerTools utilities at mcp.killertools.net. This policy covers that service, including its directory connections. The installed Windows KillerMCP app is a separate product with its own privacy policy.

Data sent to the service

Your AI client sends the tool name and the arguments selected for that call. Those arguments can contain text, structured documents, public domain names, phone numbers, email headers, or other values you deliberately supply for conversion, calculation, or lookup. Processing takes place on a Cloudflare Worker. The service does not request your full conversation, read your local files, or access your installed apps.

Cloudflare receives ordinary connection information, including your IP address and request headers, to deliver and protect the service. The Worker uses the connecting IP address to enforce a limit of 120 requests per minute. Users sharing an IP address share that limit.

Recipients and purpose

Cloudflare hosts and processes requests. DNS lookup sends the requested domain name and record type to Cloudflare DNS. RDAP lookup sends the requested public domain to the relevant registration service selected through IANA. CVE search sends the CVE identifier or search terms through the KillerTools NVD proxy. GIF search sends the search terms through the KillerTools GIF proxy. Other calculations and conversions run in the Worker without forwarding their input to those lookup providers.

FIGlet font retrieval and the KillerScripts catalog use fixed public URLs. Font requests include the selected font name; script catalog retrieval does not forward the user's search terms. Links returned by a tool are opened only when your client or you choose to open them.

Retention

The application has no account database, saved conversations, or persistent storage for tool inputs and outputs. Request data is processed for the call. CVE search terms and results may remain in the Worker's bounded, temporary memory cache, with five-minute cache reuse. That memory can remain until the cache entry is evicted or Cloudflare ends the Worker instance; five minutes is not a guaranteed deletion deadline. Catalog and font data may also remain in temporary Worker memory.

Workers Logs and Logpush are not enabled for this service. Cloudflare may separately retain network and security information under its privacy policy. External lookup providers and your AI client have their own retention policies. This service cannot delete information retained by those providers or by your AI client.

Your choices

No account is required. You can stop sending requests or disconnect the plugin at any time. Send only the information needed for the task. Do not send passwords, API keys, authentication codes, payment-card information, government identifiers, protected health information, or confidential customer records.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to steve@thekiller.net. You can report service problems through GitHub issues.
